About PerpyX

PerpyX only displays data from your Bybit account and public market data. It is not financial advice and it never places orders. Data can be delayed or incomplete — always check on Bybit before acting. Price alarms are informational: they can be delayed or missed (for example when the phone is asleep or offline), so never rely on them alone to manage risk. PerpyX is not affiliated with Bybit.
