What you get

Everything about a position, without opening the exchange app.

Built around one question: how are my positions doing right now — and how close are they to anything that matters?

Live positions

Mark price, PnL and size stream in over WebSocket. Sort by size, PnL or distance to liquidation; the card turns red when liquidation gets close.

SL / TP ladder

Every card shows entry, stop-losses and take-profits on one scale, with the price moving between them and what each level means in USDT.

Funding, honestly

Next payment and its amount, the pair's real interval, and the total paid since this position opened — not since you first traded the pair.

Alerts, even with the screen off

Hold your finger on the chart and the alert is set — or put one on a trendline you drew, or at a point in time. Our server watches the price and sends a push notification; tap it and the chart opens with your position.

Live watchlists

Your own lists as tiles with live prices and 24h sparklines — superfast, swipe between them, add any pair in a second, hold a tile to reorder. Your open positions show right on the tile.

Touch-first drawing

A crosshair you steer from anywhere on the screen, so your finger never hides the point you're placing. Trendlines, levels, channels, Fibonacci, measure — saved per pair.

A real chart

Entry, SL, TP, liquidation and open orders with clean labels that never overlap, every fill marked at its exact price, live PnL line, plus RSI, EMA, Bollinger, volume profile and more. Hide any layer for a clean view.

Trade history

One trade, one card — every partial exit on its own line with size, price, % and PnL, plus the total. Tap it and the chart zooms right into the trade, everything around it fades out.

Your setup, backed up

Sign in with a code from your email — no password. Watchlists, drawings, alerts and settings are backed up automatically and come back on a new phone. API keys never leave the device.

Designed and tested on a foldable: a compact list on the cover screen, a full-width chart when you open it up.

Security

Read-only. On purpose.

  • It can't trade. PerpyX only reads positions, orders and balances. It has no code path that places an order.
  • Keys stay on your phone. Your API key and secret are stored on the device, never on our servers.
  • Signed locally. Each request is signed on the phone; only the signature travels to the exchange, never the secret.
  • Read-only, enforced. PerpyX checks the key's permissions and refuses one that can trade or withdraw. You can revoke it on the exchange at any time.
  • Hyperliquid without a key. Just paste your public wallet address — nothing to sign, nothing that could move funds.
  • Alerts without your keys. The alert server only gets your alert levels and pairs. It watches public prices — it never sees your keys, positions or balance.
  • Optional app lock. A PIN, with your fingerprint as a shortcut, so nobody holding your unlocked phone sees your positions.
