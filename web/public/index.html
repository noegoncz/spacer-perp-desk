What you get

Everything about a position, without opening the exchange app.

Built around one question: how are my positions doing right now — and how close are they to anything that matters?

Live positions

Mark price, PnL and size stream in over WebSocket. No refresh button, no pull-to-reload.

SL / TP ladder

Every card shows entry, stop-losses and take-profits on one scale, with the price moving between them and what each level means in USDT.

Funding, honestly

Next payment and its amount, the pair's real interval, and the total paid since this position opened — not since you first traded the pair.

Touch-first drawing

A crosshair you steer from anywhere on the screen, so your finger never hides the point you're placing. Trendlines, levels, channels, Fibonacci, measure.

Price alarms

Alert on a price level, on a trendline you drew, or at a point in time. Turn any drawing into an alarm with one tap.

A real chart

Your entry, SL, TP, liquidation and open orders drawn on the chart, fills marked on the candles they happened in, plus RSI, EMA, Bollinger, volume profile and more.

Screens

Made for one hand — and for the big screen when you unfold.

Designed and tested on a foldable: a compact list on the cover screen, a full-width chart when you open it up.

Security

Read-only. On purpose.

  • It can't trade. PerpyX only reads positions, orders and balances. It has no code path that places an order.
  • Keys stay on your phone. Your API key and secret are stored on the device, never on our servers.
  • Signed locally. Each request is signed on the phone; only the signature travels to Bybit, never the secret.
  • Use a read-only key. We ask for one, and you can revoke it on Bybit at any time.
